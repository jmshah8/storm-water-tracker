Column dictionary

overflows.csv: one row per overflow ever seen

  • overflow_key: company slug and the company's overflow ID, joined by a colon.
  • company_slug, company_name: the company.
  • source_id: the company's overflow ID, as published.
  • latitude, longitude: WGS84, 6 decimal places; empty if not published.
  • receiving_watercourse: as published; may be empty.
  • first_seen_utc: when we first saw the overflow. last_seen_utc: when its published status, event times, coordinates or watercourse last changed.

events/YYYY-MM.csv: one row per discharge, filed by the UTC month it started

  • event_id: overflow_key and the published start time in epoch milliseconds, joined by a colon. Never changes.
  • overflow_key, company_slug.
  • start_utc: published start. end_utc: published end; empty while ongoing, or if the published end was earlier than the start.
  • duration_min: whole minutes from start to end; empty while there is no end.
  • source: hub for the National Storm Overflow Hub.
  • first_observed_utc, last_observed_utc: when we first saw the event, and when its row last changed.
  • end_observed: true if the end came from the feed; false if inferred from the start of the overflow's next discharge.

rain/gauges.csv: Environment Agency rain gauges with 15-minute totals

  • gauge_id: the Hydrology API station notation. measure_id: its 15-minute rainfall measure.
  • label, latitude, longitude, date_opened: as published.
  • fetched_utc: when the row was last fetched with a change.

classification/all_events_classified.csv and dry_day_spills.csv

One row per event in the first; only the dry day spills in the second.

  • event_id, overflow_key, company_slug, start_utc, end_utc, duration_min, source: copied from the events files.
  • day_utc: the UTC date the discharge started.
  • window_start_utc, window_end_utc: 00:00 UTC on the day before, to 00:00 UTC on the day after (48 hours).
  • gauge_id, gauge_label, gauge_distance_km: the gauge used; empty if none. Where three gauges were triangulated, these describe the nearest of the three.
  • gauge_method: nearest when one gauge within 10 km was used, triangulated_3 when the three closest gauges within 20 km were triangulated because none was within 10 km.
  • triangulated_gauges: for triangulated_3, the three gauges with their distances and window totals, separated by |; empty otherwise.
  • rain_day_mm: rain on day_utc. rain_prev24_mm: rain on the day before. rain_window_total_mm: the two added. rain_window_max15_mm: the largest single 15-minute reading in the window.
  • n_readings_present, n_readings_expected: 15-minute readings found, of 192.
  • n_gauges_skipped_stuck: how many nearer gauges were skipped because they had stopped reporting rain (see the Method).
  • verdict: dry_day, not_dry, pending_rain_data, no_gauge_within_10km or insufficient_readings. verdict_basis: total. rule_version: {{ rule_version }}.
  • classified_utc: when this row's classification last changed.
  • radar_window_total_mm: Met Office radar rainfall over the overflow's own 1 km square across the same 48-hour window. radar_3x3_max_total_mm: the highest of the nine squares around it. radar_status: complete (both days have at least 92 of 96 radar frames), partial (both days present, fewer frames) or missing. These are a second opinion and never change a verdict.
  • is_final: true once the verdict can no longer change (see the Method).

classification/verdict_changes.csv

  • event_id, from_verdict, to_verdict, changed_utc, n_readings_present: every change between dry_day and not_dry.
